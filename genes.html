Methods for Genetic Engineering

CRISPR-Cas9 System

CRISPR-Cas9 allows scientists to directly insert foreign DNA into plant genomes.

Agrobacterium tumefaciens

Agrobacterium acts as a delivery vehicle (through plasmids) to deliver foreign genes.

Electroporation with Foreign DNA

Cell membranes are subjected to electrical pulses, and foreign DNA enters through the resulting membrane pores.

Nanoparticle Insertion

Enzymes can be packaged in nanoparticles, bypassing the need for genome engineering.

Genes and Organism Sources

Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP)

Model Organism:
Aequorea victoria

Photoprotein aequorin emits blue light and interacts with GFP to produce fluorescence.

Luciferase (luc)

Luciferin-luciferase reaction produces yellow-green bioluminescence.

Fungi Bioluminescence Pathway

Model Organisms:
Aspergillus nidulans,
Neonothopanus nambi,
Mycena chlorophos

Includes luciferase (luz), h3h, hispS, cph, and npgA genes involved in the caffeic acid cycle.

luxCDABE Operon

Model Organism:
Photobacterium leiognathi

luxA and luxB code for luciferase subunits within the bacterial lux operon.

Coelenterazine Reaction

Model Organism:
Isidella tentaculum

Luciferase-catalyzed reaction involving coelenterazine in marine organisms.

Bioluminescent Cloud Defense

Model Organism:
Heterocarpus ensifer

Produces bioluminescent clouds through luciferase-luciferin reactions in specialized mouth glands.
