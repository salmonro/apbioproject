About Bioluminescent Plants

Ever imagined bioluminescent trees lighting up the streets, instead of street lights?
Current research focuses on introducing luciferase genes from fireflies, GFP genes from Aequorea victoria (glowing jellyfish), and similar bioluminescence genes into plants. CRISPR can prove useful in gene modification.

Problems associated with gene modification:
Modifying prompoters, transcription factors, and gene regulatory sites to ensure proper transcription of foreign bioluminescent genes engineered into plants. Addition of necessary coenzymes and cofactors.

Another lingering question: If bioluminescent plants were used as alternatives to artifical lighting, how can the timing of the glow be modified in the day versus the night?
