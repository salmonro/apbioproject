About Bioluminescent Plants

Ever imagined bioluminescent trees lighting up the streets, instead of street lights?
Current research focuses on introducing luciferase genes from fireflies, GFP genes from Aequorea victoria (glowing jellyfish), and similar bioluminescence genes into plants. CRISPR can prove useful in gene modification.

Problems associated with gene modification:
Modifying promoters, transcription factors, and gene regulatory sites to ensure proper transcription of foreign bioluminescent genes engineered into plants. Addition of necessary coenzymes and cofactors.

Another lingering question: How can the timing of the glow be modified to match natural day-night cycles?
Manipulating the molecular mechanisms of circadian regulation such that plants light up in the day, similar to lights, and turn off in the dark. First we have to ask if bioluminescence oscillates according to a 24-hour cycle, and find how we can manipulate that cycle to align with environmental signals such as light in the day and the temperature drop at night.
