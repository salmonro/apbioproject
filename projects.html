Current and Past Projects

Light Bio

LightBio sells bioluminescent plants featured on TIME Best Inventions of 2024. The company engineers glowing petunias--a type of flower-- by introducing four genes from a bioluminescent mushroom and a fifth gene from fungi into the flower.

MIT Nanobionic Plants

Instead of directly introducing genes, MIT embedded plants with nanoparticles containing luciferase, an enzyme that catalyzes a light-producing reaction, and other necessary ingredients. For greater control, the glowing plants can be turned off by adding nanoparticles with a luciferase inhibitor and necessary components.
