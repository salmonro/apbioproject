🌙 Circadian Control of Bioluminescence
Lingering question: How do we make the trees light up only at night and control its bioluminescence? MIT engineers (https://news.mit.edu/2017/engineers-create-nanobionic-plants-that-glow-1213) are planning on inserting nanoparticles with luciferase inhibitors to “turn off” bioluminescence when desired.
🌿 Plant Circadian System
Circadian system idea: - Can we utilize natural circadian rhythm systems in plants so plants “glow” at night? “Molecular clock” proteins regulate gene activation/inactivation.
