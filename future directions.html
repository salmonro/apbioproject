Future Directions

Section 1
Lingering question: How do we make trees light up only at night and control its bioluminescence? MIT engineers (MIT (2017)) are planning to insert nanoparticles with luciferase inhibitors to “turn off” bioluminescence when desired
Section 2
Circadian system idea: - Can we use natural circadian rhythm systems so plants “glow” at night and not in the day? - Can luciferase be activated at certain times and inactivated at others → light, temperature, and other conditions “Molecular clock” proteins can activate or inactivate fluorescent proteins like luciferase Specialized glands/organs that contain luciferase, oxygen, luciferin, hispidin, and components. Possibly in leaves
